About

Overview

This project predicts a student's exam score from 7 daily habits using Linear Regression. The model is trained on a dataset of 1000 students.

How it works
  1. Enter your daily habits (study hours, sleep, exercise etc.)
  2. The model predicts your score
  3. You get a score out of 100
  4. You also get some tips based on your habits
Tech Used
  • Backend: Python, Flask
  • ML Model: Linear Regression (scikit-learn)
  • Database: SQLite
  • Frontend: Bootstrap 5, Chart.js
